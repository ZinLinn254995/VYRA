VYRA · TECHNICAL OVERVIEW

DNA & Consistency: the knowledge behind every Creative Universe.

A Vyral is a reusable Creative Universe that can contain characters, worlds, locations, stories, relationships, rules, timelines, state, and creative Assets. VYRA maintains structured DNA and fingerprints behind the scenes so creators can preserve identity and continuity while creating, editing, sharing, and publishing media.

What does DNA describe?

Asset DNA records an Asset's identity, relevant properties, structure, provenance, and references. Universe DNA is derived from its Assets, entities, relationships, canon, timeline, and evolving state. It is internal structured knowledge used by VYRA's Consistency Engine, not a requirement for creators to author technical records.

One identity system, multiple evidence types

Asset identity and visual evidence

For supported PNG, JPEG and WebP uploads, VYRA uses a pinned server-side ImageMagick WASM decoder to produce a canonical row-major RGBA8 sample stream. The stream is stored in lossless tiles. Tile SHA-256 values and an aggregate decoded-sample SHA-256 digest support source integrity verification.

Important boundary: this path can recover the decoded pixel samples exactly under the recorded decoder contract and encode them into a new PNG. It does not recover the original PNG or JPEG file bytes, original compression choices, or every original metadata chunk.

Source integrity and provenance

For supported non-raster files, Asset integrity records preserve the source file's SHA-256 identity. VYRA can verify retained source bytes against that identity when assets are packaged, copied, or restored.

DNA, fingerprints, and provenance are different

  • DNA describes identity and the knowledge needed to create consistent related media.
  • Fingerprint checks whether source bytes or visual samples match recorded evidence.
  • Provenance records where an asset or observation came from and whether its claims are trusted.

A matching fingerprint is evidence of identity for the data that was hashed. It does not, by itself, prove authorship, ownership, copyright or a license grant.

How does VYRA use DNA?

When a creator asks VYRA to create or edit a scene, the Context Engine retrieves only the relevant character, world, asset, canon, relationship, and timeline knowledge. The Consistency Engine uses those references to guide generation, validate outputs, and support correction workflows. VYRA does not promise that every generative model will produce pixel-identical results.

Third-party access to private VYRA assets requires explicit, permission-controlled access. Exporting a package and granting an external service ongoing access are separate actions; a package does not silently grant account access.

Integrity, consistency, and limitations

Source integrity checks can verify hashes and supported visual fingerprints. Creative consistency is a separate challenge: generative providers can vary, so VYRA combines structured knowledge, relevant references, context retrieval, validation, and correction rather than promising perfect output from a single prompt. Imported evidence is treated as unverified until checked in the destination workspace.
